The Brand

Dust on the boots,
gold on the horizon.

Western Avenue was stitched together for women who grew up on backroads and never quite left them — the ones who pair fringe jackets with city sidewalks and don't apologize for the boots.

Every piece is built the way a good saddle is built: raw materials, real stitching, made to be lived in rather than looked at once and folded away.
